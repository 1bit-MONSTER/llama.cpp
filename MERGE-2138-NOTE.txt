## WHY llama-bench.cpp's 11 hunks are NOT a mechanical union (measured, not assumed)
Our flag is `fused_cross_device`, AMD's is `repack`, and both are threaded through eleven places -- but they are not
the same KIND of field at each of them, and the same name appears in THREE structs in AMD's file:

    cmd_params                repack is std::vector<bool>   (line 367)  -- one entry per run/device
    cmd_params_instance       repack is bool                (line 1238) -- consumed by mparams.use_extra_bufts = repack
    (result struct)           repack is bool                (line 1505) -- the column that gets printed

So in hunk 6 `mparams.use_extra_bufts = repack;` is legal because that `repack` is the instance's bool, while in hunk 1
`std::vector<bool> repack;` is the params member, and in hunk 9 `std::to_string(repack)` is the result's bool. A per-hunk
diff shows none of this: every hunk simply reads "ours' flag line vs theirs' flag line".

The union therefore has to place each flag in THREE structs (params, instance, result), in a consistent order within
each, with the default initialiser (positional!) matching the params struct order and the to_string order matching the
result struct order. That is one coordinated edit across four views of the same two flags, and it cannot be validated
by brace counting or by py_compile -- it needs a compile of the tool.

Left marked deliberately. Next attempt should start from `struct cmd_params` and walk the flag through params ->
defaults -> help -> parser -> instance -> result -> compare, adding fused_cross_device and repack together at each stop.

## src/llama-kv-cache.cpp hunk 2 (LEFT MARKED): our kv-share buffer vs AMD's owning container
AMD's refactor replaced the per-ctx allocation block with `llama_memory_bufs` (src/llama-memory.h:36), whose `emplace()`
wraps the buffer in a `ggml_backend_buffer_ptr` -- an OWNING unique_ptr -- and whose public `bufs` vector holds
`(context_ptr, buffer_ptr)` pairs. Our side of the conflict is the older, richer block: kv-share allocation
(`share_alloc`), the no_alloc dummy-buffer path, the alloc-failure check, and the engine#528 zero-fill verification
(`llama_kv_zero_fill`) that refuses a cache whose padding is not zero because every attention kernel masks those
columns with -INFINITY.

The two cannot be combined by editing text: our kv-share region is exported/aliased elsewhere (llama-kv-share.cpp) and
must NOT be freed by the cache's container, while `emplace()` takes ownership unconditionally. Options for whoever
resolves it: (a) give `llama_memory_bufs` a non-owning insertion for shared regions (e.g. an emplace_borrowed), (b) keep
our block and store the shared buffer outside `bufs` with an explicit lifetime, or (c) extend emplace() with the
kv-share branch and a flag saying the buffer is borrowed. Each changes where the free happens, so it is an ownership
decision, not a merge decision -- and the wrong choice is a double free or a leaked GTT region, neither of which a
compile catches.
